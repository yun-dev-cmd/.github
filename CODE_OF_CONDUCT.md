# Code of Conduct

This account is maintained as a practical and respectful development space.

## Expected Behavior

- Be direct, specific, and respectful.
- Discuss code, design, and product tradeoffs with evidence.
- Assume mistakes can be fixed through clear feedback.
- Keep issues and pull requests focused on the project.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or personal attacks.
- Posting private information, secrets, credentials, or personal data.
- Repeated off-topic comments that make project discussion harder.

## Enforcement

Comments or contributions that violate this code may be hidden, closed, or removed. Repeated abuse may lead to blocking from the repository.
